Ignition

A reimplementation of the 1997 racer, running in your browser. It needs the files from your own copy of the game: pick the root of the CD (or of a copy of it, the folder with BALTAZAR and LEVELS in it).

The files stay in this browser's storage and are never uploaded. CD music is not played in the browser.

Keyboard: arrows to steer and pick, Enter to choose, Escape to go back. Gamepads and touch screens also work.
